Welcome to eBanashree

More Than a Farm. More Than a Stay.

Nestled amidst the natural rolling landscape of Keonjhar, eBanashree is a 50-acre bamboo farming and eco-retreat destination created for people who want to slow down, breathe deeper, and reconnect with nature.

🌾

Sustainable Farming

Discover thousands of thriving bamboo stalks planted with care to enrich Odisha's soil, produce clean oxygen, and support sustainable local agriculture.

🛖

Authentic Eco Huts

Stay in handcrafted bamboo cottages built with organic materials, offering natural cooling, peaceful privacy, and private verandas facing green groves.

🌌

Starry Night Stays

When darkness falls, gather around the open bonfire, watch unpolluted starry skies, listen to night forest sounds, and wake up refreshed to birdsong.

Environmental Philosophy

Growing Green. Living Responsibly.

Bamboo is one of nature's fastest-growing renewable resources. eBanashree is committed to responsible land stewardship, plastic reduction, and rural community empowerment.

🌿 Carbon Absorption

Bamboo groves sequester high amounts of atmospheric carbon dioxide, creating a lush oxygen-rich microclimate across the 50 acres.

🤝 Rural Employment

eBanashree directly supports local Odisha villagers and artisans, providing dignified year-round agricultural and eco-hospitality employment.

💧 Water Conservation

Drip irrigation systems and natural soil mulch preserve rainwater run-off, maintaining soil fertility naturally without harsh chemicals.

Meet the Founder

A Dream Rooted in the Soil of Odisha

"eBanashree is not simply a place to sleep. It is a sanctuary to slow down, breathe deeply, walk among bamboo, and reconnect with what truly matters."

Minaketan Sethi envisioned eBanashree as a unique model combining large-scale bamboo farming with authentic rural eco-tourism in Keonjhar, Odisha.

By introducing guests to bamboo cultivation, peaceful cottage stays, and local culture, eBanashree fosters deep respect for nature while building long-term sustainable livelihoods for local communities.
